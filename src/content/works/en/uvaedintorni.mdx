## The brief

Every September, Uva e Dintorni brings thousands of people to Avio. The site had three jobs: make people want
to be there, tell them **what, when and where** at a glance, and work well on a phone, in a crowd, on a bad connection.

## What I did

- **No framework**: hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript. What's in the folder is exactly what's online, no build step.
- Images converted to `webp` by a script, so pages stay light even on 4G.
- English and German versions generated from the Italian one with `tools/traduci.py`: only one language is edited by hand.
- Menu and footer kept in sync across all pages by another script.

> Festival concept and visual identity by Aurora Mattei: the site follows its colours and typefaces.
