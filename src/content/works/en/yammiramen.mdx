## The brief

Yammi Ramen serves ramen, donburi and dumplings a few steps from Trento station, in an anime-inspired setting.
The website had to say quickly **what's on the menu, how much it costs and how to book**, with the same character
as the restaurant: colourful, playful, full of illustrations.

## What I did

- Design and development in **Astro**, with fast static pages: home, menu, offers, the restaurant and contacts.
- The **menu** lives in a single data file (dishes, prices, descriptions, allergens, vegetarian options): edit it
  there and the menu, the home page and the structured data for search engines update together.
- An **animated hero** with a video of the anime-style restaurant and falling petals, with a fallback button where
  the browser blocks autoplay (Safari).
- **Booking and directions in one tap**: on phones, a fixed bar with "Book" (WhatsApp) and "Directions".
- Offers as cards with highlighted prices, and the restaurant's Google rating front and centre.
- Structured data for Google (restaurant, opening hours, menu with prices), a sitemap and a redirect from the old
  address to the main domain.

## Pages

Home, Menu, Offers, The restaurant and Contacts. All light and phone-friendly, where most people looking for
somewhere to eat will arrive.
