## The brief

An architecture studio lives on images: the site had to step aside and let the projects speak, with a quiet,
recognisable identity.

## What I did

- Interface design and full development in **plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript**.
- Portfolio split by category (public, private, urban furniture, restoration), plus studio and team pages.
- Light and dark theme, and a structure that's easy to update when new work comes in.
